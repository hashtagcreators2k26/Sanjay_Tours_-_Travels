About Sanjay Tours & Travels

A Madurai-based travel service focused on comfortable journeys, personalised assistance and genuine customer care.

Our story

Travel planned around the traveller

Sanjay Tours & Travels is based in Madurai, Tamil Nadu, and works with travellers who want a journey handled with care — from a short local ride to a longer trip across the region.

Rather than offering a fixed set of packages, we start with what you actually need: where you're going, how many are travelling, and what matters most for that particular trip. The plan is built from there.

Every enquiry is handled directly and personally over WhatsApp, so you get a clear answer on availability and pricing before you commit to anything.

Our approach

How we work with every customer

Our Travel Approach

We treat each enquiry as a conversation, not a booking form — understanding your route, timing and group before suggesting a vehicle or plan.

Our Commitment

Clear communication, punctual pickups and a vehicle that matches what you told us you needed — no surprises on the day of travel.

Customer Experience

From the first WhatsApp message to the final drop-off, the goal is a journey that feels easy, unhurried and well looked after.

Why Travel With Us?

Local knowledge of Madurai and its surrounding routes, paired with a genuine interest in getting your trip right.
